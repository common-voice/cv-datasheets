The 60 questions were developed directly in Sena by the author, a native speaker. The corpus underwent orthographic normalization according to the standard of the Núcleo de Estudos das Línguas Moçambicanas (NELIMO) and was validated by 2 other native speakers from the Sofala and Tete region to ensure linguistic correctness, correct use of tones, and cultural appropriateness before being uploaded to Common Voice.
