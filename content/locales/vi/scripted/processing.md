1. The dictionary is copied page by page in the PDF format.
2. Use the online OCR to text conversion service Convertio to convert the files to a text format.
3. Process the text files using Python to extract example sentences.
4. Manually proof read the result to correct OCR typos and remove extra sentences.
5. Due to Common Voice limits, only sentences with 5 or more words and less than 15 words are submitted.
